Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the practices described below.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register, along with the expense data you choose to log — amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and usage activity, to help us maintain and improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain your account and expense records
  • Generate spending summaries, charts, and category breakdowns for you
  • Communicate with you about your account or changes to the Service
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or misuse

We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and protected using reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. Passwords are never stored in plain text. You may request export or deletion of your data at any time through your account settings; deleted data is permanently removed from our active systems, except where retention is required for legal or security reasons. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal expense data with advertisers or data brokers. We may use limited third-party services (such as hosting or analytics providers) solely to operate the Service, and any such providers are required to handle your data responsibly and only as instructed by us.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact details provided on your account page.
